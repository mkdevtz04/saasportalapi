# TrinetPay: product specification

This is what the platform is and the decisions behind it. How it works inside is in
[docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md). Earlier versions of this file described a plan (Filament panels,
monthly subscriptions, per-ISP payment keys). Those were dropped, and this file now matches what was built.

## What it is

A hotspot billing platform for small ISPs in Tanzania and East Africa, priced in TZS and built around local mobile
money. An ISP signs up, connects a MikroTik router with one pasted command, sets its packages and payout number,
and has a working portal at its own address (`wifikitaa.site/portal/acme`).

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Price | **Free.** No subscription, no trial, no monthly fee. |
| Revenue | A percentage of each **withdrawal** (`PLATFORM_WITHDRAWAL_FEE_PCT`). Nothing at payment time, nothing on vouchers. |
| Payments | One platform PalmPesa account collects every portal payment. Each ISP has a wallet and withdraws to its own mobile money number. |
| Vouchers | Generated by the ISP, sold for cash directly or through agents. Cash the ISP already holds: recorded for reports, never credited to the wallet. |
| Routers | Any MikroTik, any RouterOS version, including v6. The router connects out to the platform. |
| Customer login | RADIUS (FreeRADIUS reading the platform database). |
| Tenancy | Shared database, every tenant-owned row carries `tenant_id`, enforced in the models. |
| Languages | Swahili and English on the customer portal, chosen per ISP with a customer override. |

## What an ISP can do

- Sign up, follow a three-step setup (router, packages, payout number).
- Add routers with one command, see whether they are online, reboot them, disconnect everyone.
- Create packages with price, time, speed and optional data cap.
- Generate and print voucher batches. Create agents who sell vouchers from their wallet balance.
- See who is online now and how much data is used, sales reports by type, package, router and day, and download
  them for Excel.
- See every payment, find one by the reference a customer reads out, and see which customers paid but are not online.
- Request withdrawals and see the fee before confirming.
- Brand the portal: logo, colour, tagline, default language.
- Get an email and SMS when a router goes offline and when it returns.

## What the platform admin can do

- See every ISP, suspend or activate one (a suspended ISP loses customer logins at once).
- Approve, pay or reject withdrawals, with a warning when the payout number differs from the saved one.
- Check reconciliation: expected PalmPesa balance, money owed, wallets that payments cannot explain.
- Download confirmed payments with PalmPesa order ids to check against the PalmPesa statement.
- Read the audit trail. Open an ISP dashboard as support (recorded, money actions blocked).

## Not built

- Subscriptions, invoices and billing cycles (dropped by decision).
- Two-factor sign-in for the platform admin (recommended before launch).
- Airtel Money and M-Pesa direct integrations (PalmPesa only for now).
- Time that starts at first login (packages start when the customer pays or redeems).
- Instant disconnect of a customer (happens within a minute, through the router agent).
